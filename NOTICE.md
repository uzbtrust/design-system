Muallifni ko‘rsatish. Muallif so‘ragan shakl:

> Dizayn: Abdurakhmonov — https://github.com/uzbtrust/design-system

- Saytda va ilovada — `Signature` imzosi: u shu matnni va repoga havolani o‘zi beradi.
